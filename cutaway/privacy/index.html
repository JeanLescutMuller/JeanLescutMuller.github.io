Cutaway · Privacy policy

Cutaway is a free app that shows, on a map of Paris, where films and series were shot, and lets you watch a film's scenes where they were shot. It is published by Jean Lescut-Muller, an individual developer.

In short

Cutaway collects no personal data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and the developer runs no server: the database of films and places is built into the app.

Your location

If you tap "near me" and allow it, the app reads your phone's location to show the film locations around you. Your location stays on your phone: it is never sent to the developer, never stored and never shared. You can refuse or withdraw this permission at any time in your phone's settings; the rest of the app works without it.

What is stored on your phone

Only your choice of language (English or French), so the app remembers it. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Outside services the app loads content from

To show the map, videos and photos, your phone downloads them directly from these services. As with any website, they receive your IP address and basic technical information (such as the type of device) when your phone asks them for content. The developer receives nothing from them.
